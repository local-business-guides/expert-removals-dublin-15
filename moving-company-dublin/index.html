What to prepare and how the move works

The exact work depends on the size of your move and the help you want. Households may want support with packing, moving furniture and boxes, or storing items temporarily. Businesses can arrange office moves, and the company also provides moving and storage services.

Before the move, it’s useful to:

  • Set aside items you’ll need on the first day at your new address.
  • Identify furniture and belongings that need extra care.
  • Decide whether you’ll pack yourself or ask about packing support.
  • Ask about storage if your move-out and move-in dates don’t line up.
  • Confirm the arrangements and any details that could affect the work.

A common mistake is assuming every item will be ready to go without checking what needs to be packed or protected. Another is leaving a storage decision until the last moment. A conversation with a Moving Company Dublin residents are considering can help clarify these points before moving day.
