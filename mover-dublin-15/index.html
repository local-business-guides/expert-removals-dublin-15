Care for furniture and belongings

Let the team know what you want moved, what you’d like help packing, and whether anything should be stored.

It’s worth mentioning delicate or unusually large pieces rather than assuming they’ll be obvious on the day. You can also ask what you should prepare yourself. Agreeing on the division of tasks early helps avoid last-minute confusion about boxes, furniture and items that aren’t going to the new home.

A simple checklist before moving day

A little preparation can make it easier to explain your needs and keep track of loose ends. Before you speak with a mover, consider:

  • Which rooms and belongings are part of the move.
  • What furniture may need extra care.
  • Whether you want the packing service.
  • If any items need to go into storage.
  • What you need to keep accessible during the move.

One mistake to avoid is treating every belonging as if it’s going to the same place. Set aside anything you plan to store or keep with you, then tell the removals team how you want it handled. If you’re not sure how to organise that, ask before moving day.
