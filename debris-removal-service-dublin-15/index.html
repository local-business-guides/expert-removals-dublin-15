Prepare before the collection

A few minutes of preparation can make it easier to explain the task and help the visit run smoothly. Use this checklist:

  • Write down the main types of material and your best estimate of the amount.
  • Note where it is located, such as inside the home, at a workplace or in another part of the property.
  • Check whether anything needs to be kept, separated or handled differently.
  • Make sure you can point out the items clearly when discussing the job.
  • Ask what information Expert Removals needs before confirming a time.

Avoid putting items into an unmarked pile if you still need to sort through them. Also avoid booking based on a vague description, then expecting the removal team to know what you meant. If the amount or type changes, contact the company and update the details before the appointment.
