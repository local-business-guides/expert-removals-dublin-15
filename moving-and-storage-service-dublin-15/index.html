Make a plan before booking

Before arranging anything, gather the information you’re likely to need. This short checklist can help you prepare:

  • Write down your current address, destination and any expected storage period.
  • Note the date you need to leave and when you expect to receive your belongings.
  • Make an approximate list of what is moving, including larger furniture.
  • Set aside items you’ll need during the gap, such as everyday essentials.
  • Ask how packing can fit into the plan if you want help preparing your belongings.

A common mistake is to focus only on the moving date and leave the storage timing undecided. Another is to pack everything together without keeping essential items easy to reach. Think about what you’ll need while your belongings are in storage, and tell Expert Removals if the dates or destination change.
